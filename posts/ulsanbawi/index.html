Hiking to the Ulsanbawi Observation Deck in Seoraksan National Park

daylight

Can visit anytime

A visual demo · a mood note, not a factual place guide

Perched high along the granite ridges of Seoraksan National Park, the Ulsanbawi observation platform delivers sweeping views across one of South Korea's most iconic rock formations. The massive pale peaks rise dramatically above densely forested green valleys under an open sky. Getting here involves a steep climb up hundreds of metal staircases, but the rugged panorama makes every step worthwhile. With only a lone hiker taking in the vista, the morning atmosphere feels peaceful, expansive, and delightfully remote.

Photo Spot Tip

Stand near the edge of the stone platform by the wooden railing, framing your subject looking out toward the jagged spires of Ulsanbawi with the deep green valley rolling behind it.

Travel Tips

  • Start your trek early in the morning to enjoy cooler temperatures and avoid crowds on the narrow staircases.
  • Wear sturdy hiking shoes with good traction, as the smooth granite rock surfaces require careful footing.
  • Bring at least one liter of water and a light windbreaker, as the exposed observation deck can get breezy.
  • Take steady breaks on the final staircase ascent to manage your pacing and stay safe.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the hike to Ulsanbawi take?

The round-trip hike typically takes between 3 to 4.5 hours from the park entrance at Sogongwon, depending on your pace.

Is the trail to Ulsanbawi difficult?

The first section to Heundeulbawi is moderate, but the final push consists of steep, continuous metal stairs that demand good physical stamina.

What is the best time of year to hike this trail?

Autumn in October offers peak fall foliage, while clear days in late spring and early summer provide excellent visibility and lush greenery.

Are there restrooms or food stands along the route?

Restrooms and small rest stops are available near Sinheungsa Temple and Heundeulbawi, but there are no facilities at the summit observation deck.

Do I need a permit or fee to access the trail?

No hiking permit is required, and national park admission is free, though parking fees apply at the Sogongwon lot.
