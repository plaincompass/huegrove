Morning arrived slowly, slipping across tiled roofs and peach-colored walls.

Somewhere nearby, coffee was being poured. A window opened. A curtain moved in the breeze, and the whole street seemed to take one unhurried breath.

I remember the color more than the map: a soft, sunlit terracotta that followed me home.
