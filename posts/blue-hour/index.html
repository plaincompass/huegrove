A visual demo · a mood note, not a factual place guide





Incheon Grand Park is home to two mountains, one of which is Mt. Gwanmo.

If you take a slow walk through the Forest Healing Center, you'll find beautiful birch trees lining the trail. The peaceful atmosphere and fresh forest air make it a perfect place to relax and reconnect with nature.



For the best photos, choose light and soft tones such as beige, ivory, or light gray. These colors harmonize beautifully with the birch trees and the gentle mood of the forest. Dark colors, especially black, tend to stand out too strongly against the natural surroundings.
