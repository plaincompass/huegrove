A visual demo · a mood note, not a factual place guide

The day loosened its hold on the shore one shade at a time.

Everything turned blue: the water, the little boats, even the quiet between one wave and the next. We stayed until the lamps along the promenade began to glow like small, patient moons.

No plans, no hurry. Just the soft feeling of being exactly where the evening wanted us.
