A small station in the hills · An almost-rainy afternoon

The green between trains

fresh leaves · distant rails · a pocket of quiet

A visual demo · a mood note, not a factual place guide

We got off one stop early, mostly because the light through the train window looked too lovely to pass by.

The station was small enough to hear leaves turning in the wind. Beyond the platform, the hills held every shade of green. A train came and went; the afternoon stayed.

Sometimes the detour is the place.
